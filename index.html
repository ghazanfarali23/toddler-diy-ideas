🛡️ Grown-up ground rules

  • Craft together — a grown-up handles scissors, cutters and hot glue.
  • Small parts stay supervised — bottle caps and googly eyes are for gluing, not tasting.
  • Seal sensory bottles tight — tape or glue the cap so curious hands can't open them.
  • Keep it non-toxic — wash bottles well and use kid-safe paint and glue.
